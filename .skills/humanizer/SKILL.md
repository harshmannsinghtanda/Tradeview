# Humanizer Copywriting & UX Microcopy Skill

A guidance framework for purging AI robotic tropes, stiffness, and corporate jargon, transforming written content and UX copy into authentic, engaging, warm, human-centric language.

---

## 1. The Banned AI Vocabulary (Words to Eradicate)

Never use these cliché AI tropes in user-facing copy or documentation:

| Banned AI Words | Natural Human Alternatives |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Delve** / **Delve into** | Explore, look at, break down, check out |
| **Testament to** | Shows, proves, reflects |
| **Rich tapestry** | Story, background, variety |
| **Seamless** / **Seamlessly** | Smooth, effortless, simple, easy |
| **Elevate** | Improve, boost, raise, upgrade |
| **Game-changer** | Huge improvement, breakthrough, big leap |
| **Leverage** | Use, take advantage of, tap into |
| **Unlock** | Gain access to, discover, open up |
| **Fostering** / **Spearheading** | Building, leading, driving, starting |
| **In conclusion** / **Furthermore** | So, wrapping up, also, plus |

## 2. Core Principles of Human Writing

### A. Vary Sentence Length & Rhythm
AI tends to write sentences of uniform, monotonous length. Humans write with rhythm:
- *Stiff*: "Our advanced trading journal platform enables users to efficiently track positions while delivering comprehensive analytics for optimal decision making."
- *Human*: "Track your trades without the headache. Spot patterns in seconds, learn from mistakes, and double down on what works."

### B. Speak Directly to the Person ("You" & "We")
- Write like a knowledgeable, friendly peer having a conversation over coffee.
- Use active voice: "We built this to save you time" instead of "Time savings are facilitated by this solution."

### C. Empathy in Failure States
- Never blame the user ("Invalid input provided by user").
- Be reassuring and clear on next steps ("We couldn't find that trade. Check the ticker symbol and try again.").

## 3. UX Microcopy Standards

### Buttons & Actions
- **Be Specific**: Action verb + object.
  - ❌ *Submit* → ✅ *Save Trade*
  - ❌ *Continue* → ✅ *Proceed to Checkout*
  - ❌ *Delete* → ✅ *Yes, delete position*

### Empty States
- Acknowledge the clean slate, explain value, give 1 primary action button:
  - *Title*: "No trades recorded yet"
  - *Subtitle*: "Log your first trade to start tracking your win rate and risk-reward ratio."
  - *Button*: "+ Log Your First Trade"

### Error Toast Notifications
- State what happened naturally and how to resolve it:
  - ❌ *Error 401: Unauthorized access token dynamic failure.*
  - ✅ *Your session expired. Please log back in to keep trading.*

## 4. Humanizer Transformation Examples

### Example 1: Feature Overview
- **AI Raw**: "This cutting-edge trading journal leverages AI algorithms to seamlessly elevate your financial performance and unlock strategic insights."
- **Humanized**: "A simple, powerful journal that turns your raw trading history into actionable insights—so you can trade smarter, not harder."

### Example 2: Onboarding Header
- **AI Raw**: "Embark on a transformative journey towards mastering market volatility."
- **Humanized**: "Welcome aboard. Let's get your trading journal set up in under 2 minutes."
